## Today I Learned

### k-Anonymity Limitations

k-anonymity guarantees each record is indistinguishable from k-1 others, but it's vulnerable to homogeneity attacks (all k records have the same sensitive value) and background knowledge attacks. l-diversity and t-closeness address these gaps.
