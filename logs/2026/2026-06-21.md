### Probabilistic Matching Thresholds

In production identity resolution, the match threshold isn't fixed — it varies by use case. Marketing (tolerance for false positives): 0.6-0.7. Financial services (zero tolerance): 0.95+. Healthcare (HIPAA): 0.99+. The threshold is a product decision, not an engineering one.
