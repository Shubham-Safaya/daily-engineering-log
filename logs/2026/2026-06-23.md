### Data Clean Room Architectures

Three clean room architectures: (1) Centralized (Snowflake, LiveRamp) — data moves to a shared environment. (2) Federated (Google Ads Data Hub) — queries move to the data. (3) Cryptographic (Duality, Cape Privacy) — computation on encrypted data. Each trades off usability vs privacy guarantees.
