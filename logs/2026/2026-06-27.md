### UID 2.0 Architecture

UID 2.0 (The Trade Desk) replaces third-party cookies with a hashed, encrypted email-based identifier. Key properties: (1) deterministic matching, (2) user opt-out via Transparency Portal, (3) regular key rotation for security. Open-source and governed by Prebid.
