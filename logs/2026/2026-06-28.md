## Today I Learned

### Propensity Score Matching

PSM creates a pseudo-randomized experiment from observational data by matching treated/control units on their propensity to receive treatment. Critical for retail media measurement when true experiments aren't feasible. Caliper width and balance diagnostics determine quality.
