## Today I Learned

### Differential Privacy

The Laplace mechanism adds noise proportional to sensitivity/epsilon. Lower epsilon = more privacy but noisier results. Apple uses epsilon=2-8 for keyboard analytics; Google uses epsilon=1-9 for Chrome.
