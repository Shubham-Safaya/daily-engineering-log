### Incrementality Testing

The gold standard for ad measurement is incrementality (causal lift), not attribution. Run a randomized holdout: show ads to treatment group, PSAs to control, measure purchase rate difference. The incremental ROAS is typically 30-60% lower than attributed ROAS.
